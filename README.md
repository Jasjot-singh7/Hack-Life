PCB Week 1:

This PCB acts as a dedicated hardware hub for life-automation projects. Instead of relying on a messy web of jumper wires and breadboards, this custom board consolidates sensors, microcontrollers, and power management into a clean, compact form factor. 
The board functions as a bridge between physical sensors and your automation software framework. 
Dedicated volume knobs, play/pause switches, and instant microphone mute buttons that interface directly with your operating system's audio mixer.
